Motor Own Damage Insurance Policy

Coverage:

1. Accidental Damage
The policy covers damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidents, including collision, overturning, and impact with another vehicle or object.

2. Fire and Explosion
The policy covers accidental loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by fire, explosion, self-ignition, or lightning.

3. Theft
The policy covers loss of the insured vehicle due to theft or attempted theft.

4. Natural Calamities
The policy covers damage caused by events such as flood, storm, cyclone, earthquake, and landslide.

5. Claim Assessment
The claim amount is subject to policy coverage, deductibles, depreciation, applicable exclusions, and the assessed repair cost.

Exclusions:

1. Normal wear and tear is not covered.

2. Mechanical or electrical breakdown without an insured accident is not covered.

3. Driving without a valid driving licence is not covered.

4. Damage caused while the vehicle is being used for an unauthorized purpose is not covered.

5. Damage caused intentionally by the insured is not covered.

6. Claims arising from driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs are not covered.

Deductible:

The applicable compulsory deductible must be deducted from the assessed claim amount before settlement.

Decision Guidelines:

A claim may be considered eligible when the reported incident is covered by the policy and none of the applicable exclusions are triggered.

A claim may be rejected when the reported incident falls under an explicit policy exclusion.
